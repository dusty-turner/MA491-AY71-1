The Blueprint
The Question

A helicopter fleet is asked to do two things that fight each other. Fly it hard and it earns the training hours the unit is graded on. Fly it hard and parts wear out, so the readiness rate the unit is also graded on falls.

The Model

Readiness and utilization come out of one coupled system, so I model them jointly rather than optimizing either alone:

\[ \max_{u_t} \; \sum_{t=1}^{T} \big[\, v(u_t) - c\big(E[D_{t+1} \mid u_t]\big) \,\big] \]

where \(u_t\) is aircraft flown on day \(t\), \(v(\cdot)\) the training value earned, and \(D_{t+1}\) the downtime that flying induces tomorrow. The interesting behavior lives in that lag: today's value is certain, tomorrow's cost is not.
